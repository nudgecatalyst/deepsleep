Six levers

Every ritual pulls one of six levers.

Sleep science points to a handful of inputs the nervous system responds to. The playbook sorts every ritual by the lever it pulls, so you can find the one that fits your day instead of copying someone else's whole routine.

Try one lever now

Breathe with the circle for one minute.

Breathe in

Box breathing: four counts in, hold four, four out, hold four. One of the oldest wind-down tools there is.

Light

Morning sun, evening amber and dimmed screens that set the body clock.

Temperature

Warm skin, cool core. The thermal cues that open the door to deep sleep.

Breath

Short, repeatable patterns that calm the nervous system in under two minutes.

Body

Walks, stretches and body scans that close the day in the muscles, not only the mind.

Sound

The playlists, silences and voices people use to tell themselves the day is done.

Closure

A line written, a list parked, a door closed. A clear ending for the day.

Why this exists

It started at home, at 3 AM.

At home

Two years ago, I began noticing something unsettling. My spouse struggled to sleep night after night. My father woke repeatedly through the night. My mother-in-law silently battled chronic insomnia that slowly drained her energy and mood.

Everyone looked functional. Very few were truly rested.

Two years of testing

That realisation became an obsession. I studied sleep behaviour, nervous system regulation, light, breath and the psychology of modern exhaustion. I tested small, practical changes at home. Some worked. Many did not. Slowly, patterns emerged.

The sleep lab

When my wife needed a hospital sleep study, the waiting list stretched close to two months. That is when it hit me: we are living through a silent sleep crisis, and most of the help on offer is clinical, expensive or impossible to sustain.

The rituals that work are rarely in articles. They live inside other people's evenings.

The playbook

So I am going to the people who have figured it out. One hundred of them, across Bengaluru. Not a medical solution, not another wellness trend. A playbook of what actually works, from the people it works for.

Honest answers

What you may be wondering.

This is a work in progress, and we would rather be clear about that than oversell it.

What exactly is the SLEAP Playbook?

A collection of the real, private rituals that 100 accomplished people in Bengaluru use to switch off, sleep, wake and recharge. Each ritual is decoded with the science behind it and broken down so you can borrow the parts that fit your life.

When does it go live?

Conversations are under way now. The playbook goes live once enough of them are decoded to be genuinely useful. Waitlist members hear first, the day it is ready.

What format will it take?

The final format is still taking shape. Whatever form best captures the collective wisdom of these conversations, the waitlist receives it right after the contributors.

Who are the 100?

People across Bengaluru who carry serious responsibility and still find a way to rest well. Founders and CEOs. Surgeons and doctors. Civil servants and politicians. Investors and traders. Scientists, judges, pilots, athletes, artists, and the parents and caregivers who hold everything together. Different lives, one shared question: how do you switch off?

Can I share my own ritual, or nominate someone?

Yes. Tick the boxes in the waitlist form. If you know someone whose calm seems almost unfair, we would love an introduction.

Is this medical advice?

No. The playbook documents lived rituals and explains the science behind them. If you have a diagnosed sleep disorder such as insomnia or sleep apnoea, please keep working with your doctor. These rituals can complement treatment, not replace it.

What happens to my details?

They are used only to tell you about the playbook. Never sold, never shared. One click and you are off the list.

Why the name SLEAP?

Sleep, with a leap in the middle. The rituals in this playbook are small, but borrowing the right one can be a short, deliberate jump from the way you sleep now to the way your body already knows how to.

There is a quieter meaning too. A group of leopards is called a leap: still and deeply at rest, then fully alert when it matters. That is the kind of rest this playbook is about.

And practically, sleap.in was the shortest domain we could find that sounds closest to the thing it stands for: sleep.
